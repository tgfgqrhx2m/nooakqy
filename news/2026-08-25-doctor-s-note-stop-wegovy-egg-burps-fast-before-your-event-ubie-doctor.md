Why Does Wegovy Cause Burps?

Wegovy is a glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor agonist. It works by slowing gastric emptying and reducing appetite. Slower stomach emptying can lead to:

Increased fermentation of food in the stomach

Greater gas production

Belching and egg-like burps

When proteins such as eggs break down, they can release sulfur-containing compounds, creating that familiar "rotten egg" smell.

Understanding Sulfur Burps

Sulfur burps are belches that carry the scent of hydrogen sulfide gas. They can be triggered by:

High-sulfur foods (eggs, broccoli, onions)

Overgrowth of certain gut bacteria

Rapid eating or swallowing air

Side effects of medications (like Wegovy)

Effective sulfur burps treatment combines dietary tweaks, lifestyle changes and, when needed, over-the-counter remedies.
