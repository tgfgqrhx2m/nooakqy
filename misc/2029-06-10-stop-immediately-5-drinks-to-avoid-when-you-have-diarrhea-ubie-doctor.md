Which drinks should be avoided when suffering from diarrhea?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

When you have diarrhea, avoid alcohol, sugary drinks, and high-caffeine beverages, as these can worsen dehydration and further irritate your digestive system. Instead, choose water, clear broths, or oral rehydration solutions to replenish lost fluids and electrolytes.

Diarrhea can stem from many causes—viral infections, food intolerances, medications, or underlying conditions—so knowing what's driving your symptoms is key to feeling better fast.
