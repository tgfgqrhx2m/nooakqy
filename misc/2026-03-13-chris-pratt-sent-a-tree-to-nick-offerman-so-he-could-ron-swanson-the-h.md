# Chris Pratt Sent a Tree to Nick Offerman So He Could 'Ron Swanson the Hell Out of It'

Let's face it: As much as we love Chris Pratt, Chris Hemsworth, Chris Pine, and Chris Evans, it can be hard to tell the Hollywood actors apart. Besides having the same first name, they all star in our favorite superhero and sci-fi movies; their fitness games are all on-point; if you blurred your eyes and looked at the four of them, you'd be hard-pressed to tell which Chris was which.

To help you keep track of everyone's favorite Chrises, we're rounding up what each of them got up to during the week. Welcome to This Week in Chrises.

Chris Pratt: Saves a felled tree and sent it to Nick Offerman

Chris Pratt must be really pumped for Nick Offerman's new crafting show.

On Instagram this week, Pratt explained how a massive 181-year-old Douglas Fir was struck by lightning on his land. Following the incident, the tree began to rot. So, like any normal human, he cut that sucker down and sent it to Nick Offerman so he could "Ron Swanson the hell out of it and make me the dopest table in history fit for a family of Vikings."

Chris Hemsworth: Showed off his dancing skills (again)

On Wednesday, Chris Hemsworth celebrated wife Elsa Pataky's birthday like any man should: By dancing like a fool.

"Gave my wife a dance lesson for her birthday, I should’ve given myself one too," Hemsworth wrote on Instagram. "Happy Birthday love."

Chris Pine: Stepped out with his new girlfriend and yet another vintage car
