The official name for winstrol is stanozolol. It was first produced by a company called Winthrop Laboratories in the 1960’s and, at this time was approved by the FDA for human consumption.

Stanozolol is predominantly a cutting steroid, due to its fat-burning effects. Winstrol burns fat by slashing cortisol levels – the hormone responsible for stubborn fat storage.
