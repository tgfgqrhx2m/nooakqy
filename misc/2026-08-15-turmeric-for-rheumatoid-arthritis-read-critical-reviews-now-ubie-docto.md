Turmeric contains curcumin, a natural compound with anti-inflammatory properties that may help reduce joint pain, stiffness, and swelling associated with rheumatoid arthritis (RA). Some studies suggest curcumin can be as effective as certain anti-inflammatory medications, though larger clinical trials are still needed to confirm its long-term benefits and ideal dosage.

If you're experiencing joint pain, stiffness, or swelling, it's important not to guess. RA symptoms often overlap with other conditions, and early identification is key to preventing joint damage.
