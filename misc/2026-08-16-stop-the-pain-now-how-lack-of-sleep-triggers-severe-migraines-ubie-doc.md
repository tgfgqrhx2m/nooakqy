Published on: 7/21/2026

Can lack of sleep trigger or worsen migraines?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Sleep deprivation is a well-known migraine trigger. Not getting enough sleep can both cause migraines and make existing ones more severe or frequent.

How Sleep Loss Triggers Migraines:

Disrupts brain chemicals that regulate pain

Increases stress hormones like cortisol

Lowers the brain's threshold for pain

Interferes with the body's natural recovery processes

Migraine Triggers: Not sleeping well can be a trigger for migraines, just like certain foods or stress.
