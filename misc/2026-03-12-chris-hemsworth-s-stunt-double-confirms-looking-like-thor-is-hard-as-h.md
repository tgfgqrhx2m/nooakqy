# Chris Hemsworth's Stunt Double Confirms Looking Like Thor Is Hard as Hell

It's hard enough for your average dude to achieve the god-like physique of Chris Hemsworth. For Bobby Holland Hanton, it's a full-time job.

The Australian stunt man has doubled for the god of thunder across two Thor movies and a trio of Avengers films, as well as Hemsworth's lesser known roles like The Huntsman: Winter's War and In The Heart of the Sea. It sounds like a cushy gig, until you realize how much effort Chris Hemsworth puts into looking like Chris Hemsworth.

"Thor is definitely the most difficult character that we have to get in shape for," Hanton — who has also worked on HBO's Game of Thrones and doubled for two of Hollywood's non-Australian Chrises, Pine and Evans — told news.com.au. "Chris is already huge. He's much bigger than me naturally, so I have to train twice a day."

The marathon filming schedule between last year's Thor: Ragnarok and the double-filming of Avengers 3 and 4 saw Hanton maintaining his Hemsworth body for a year and a half. This meant two-a-days in the gym, and a restrictive diet that the stunt man stuck to alongside the actor.

“We might train first thing in the morning and last thing at night," Hanton said. "We try and eat every two-and-a-half hours. For breakfast we might have something like scrambled eggs with steak and spinach. No carbs in the morning. Then two hours later we might have a chicken breast and salad."

“After our first training session we’d introduce the carbs, usually brown rice, sweet potato or quinoa and then we’d continue to eat carbs up until the last meal. It’s pretty intense.”

By the sound of it, Chris Hemsworth's body causes more overall issues in the MCU than anything cooked up by Josh Brolin's Thanos. (Although in fairness, Brolin is yoked as hell, too.) John Krasinski — who may just be next in line to throw on some Marvel-branded spandex — once revealed that the sight of Hemsworth's physique put him off playing a superhero for years.
