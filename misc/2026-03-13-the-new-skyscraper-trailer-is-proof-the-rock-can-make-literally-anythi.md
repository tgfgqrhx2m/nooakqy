# The New 'Skyscraper' Trailer Is Proof The Rock Can Make Literally Anything Look Cool

Since departing the pro wrestling squared circle for Hollywood, Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson has made a career out of consistently spinning low-to-mid level ideas into ass-kicking, box-office-busting dynamite.

Remake of a beloved Robin Williams board game adventure? Amazing! Reboot of an arcade game you probably last played blacked out at an arcade bar? Sounds good! A full length action film based on a 50-year-old Disney ride where a riverboat captain points at animatronic hippos? It will probably make 1 trillion dollars and you'll see it twice.

The latest and greatest example of The Rock's movie magic dropped today in the form of a trailer for Skyscraper, a movie that appears to care very little for physics or reason but nevertheless looks like the dopest thing since Die Hard.

Johnson plays Will Sawyer, a former FBI Hostage Rescue agent who now inspects skyscrapers for danger. In the middle of assessing the biggest skyscraper, terrorists set the 96th floor ablaze, trapping Sawyer's family above the fire.

Naturally, The Rock makes this work. The Rock makes everything work. Here is a brief rundown of some very suspect things in the Skyscraper trailer that The Rock manages to make cool.

[The New Skyscraper' Trailer](https://synaptide-lab.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/7641557075.html)

Being a building inspector

One of the best parts of the trailer is The Rock heading to his mid-level corporate job with an adorable sports jacket and shoulder bag with no one wondering why he's built like an old-timey circus strongman ate a tank. Imagine if your office health inspector showed up to check the carbon monoxide alarm and looked like Dwayne-The-Damn-Rock-Johnson.

Flailing wildly

Not a single other action star could pull off this dopey-looking affront to modern physics off a super-crane without it being embarrassing. Maybe Tom Cruise, but that dude would've tried it for real and then we'd mostly be talking about how Tom Cruise is dead now.

[New Skyscraper'](https://jointrevive.xianfengchem.com/)

Having a prosthetic leg

Reader, The Rock uses his own prosthetic leg to stop a door from closing, and it is already the most absurd action movie moment in at least 35 years. I am sold, somehow, on this ridiculous thing.
