# Warning: Critical Acetaminophen Side Effects to Watch For Now | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/21/2026

What side effects should I watch for when taking acetaminophen?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Acetaminophen is generally safe when used correctly, but taking too much can cause serious liver damage or even liver failure. To avoid an overdose, always follow the dosage instructions on the label, don't combine multiple medications containing acetaminophen, and limit alcohol use while taking it. Adults should typically not exceed 4,000 mg in 24 hours, and less may be safer for some individuals.
