Sleep myoclonus is a condition marked by sudden, involuntary muscle jerks that occur during sleep or as you're drifting off. While the exact triggers aren't always well-documented, common contributing factors may include stress, caffeine intake, sleep deprivation, certain medications, and underlying neurological conditions. Episodes can range from harmless "sleep starts" to more disruptive patterns that affect sleep quality.
