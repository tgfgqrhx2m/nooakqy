# Arnold Says 'Control Freak' James Cameron Is Still Involved in 'Terminator 6'

Arnold Schwarzenegger has confirmed that James Cameron, the director of The Terminator and Terminator 2: Judgment Day, is still heavily involved in the film series he created, despite commitments to his other epic franchise, Avatar.

In a fan site interview, Schwarzenegger revealed that “control freak” Cameron made sure to find time to oversee filming of the upcoming sixth film in the Terminator series, known as Terminator: Dark Fate.

“Jim Cameron is right now directing Avatar films, so he’s taken on this enormous challenge and he’s very busy with that, BUT he’s a control freak,” Schwarzenegger said. “As you know, Terminator is kind of his baby; so he does get involved in the filming.”

This resulted in a collaborative filmmaking process with director Tim Miller (best known for his work on Deadpool). Arnie continued: “There were interesting discussions about which direction to go with a certain scene or how the dialogue should finish or what the certain look should be of an individual and all that stuff, so there was a big collaboration between Jim and Tim Miller. I feel it was in good hands with both of them.”

Fans have reason to get even more excited than usual for Terminator 6, as it marks the return of Linda Hamilton to the franchise. This will be her first appearance as Sarah Connor since 1991’s Judgment Day.

Connor was played by Game of Thrones star Emilia Clarke in 2015’s Terminator: Genisys, however, the actress has since said she will not be returning to the franchise, and it appears that Terminator 6 will be a direct sequel to Judgment Day, possibly retconning the events of later films and TV spinoff The Sarah Connor Chronicles.

A publicity image for the film showed Hamilton and co-stars Natalia Reyes and Mackenzie Davis in character, suggesting that Terminator 6 will introduce a new generation of action heroines.

Terminator 6 will be released on November 1.
