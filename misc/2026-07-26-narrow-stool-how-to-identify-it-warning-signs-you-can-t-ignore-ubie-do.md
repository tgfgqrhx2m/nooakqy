Narrow stool, sometimes described as "pencil-thin," refers to bowel movements that appear thinner in width than usual. While the change can be noticeable, narrow stool is not necessarily a sign of a specific health condition. In many cases, it results from temporary factors such as diet, fiber intake, or stress. However, persistent narrow stools may occasionally indicate an underlying issue, such as constipation, hemorrhoids, or, less commonly, a blockage in the colon.
