(Judging by the hype, the iPhone 8 will be no exception.) However, the latest Apple object of everyone's desire isn't particularly sleek and futuristic. In fact, it's a chunky, retro edition that is positively dated by today's standards of design.

We're referring to this pair of Apple Sneakers, which is being auctioned off for $15,000 by Heritage M&C Art. They're big, all-white low-tops with what looks like solid heel cushioning and a puffiness typical of many '90s sneakers. And it's their vintage appeal and novelty that is surely what's driving that price tag up. For reference, the latest pair of Yeezy Powerphase Calabasas sneakers, which bear some resemblance to these kicks, retailed for $120. Perhaps Apple's original rainbow logo just has that much more cachet than Adidas' three stripes.
