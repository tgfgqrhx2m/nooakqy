Common sleep disorders include insomnia, sleep apnea, restless legs syndrome (RLS), and narcolepsy.

Insomnia: Difficulty falling or staying asleep.

Sleep apnea: Breathing repeatedly stops and starts during sleep.

Restless legs syndrome: An uncontrollable urge to move the legs, usually at night.

Narcolepsy: Excessive daytime sleepiness and sudden sleep attacks.

Each of these conditions can significantly impact sleep quality, daytime energy, mood, and long-term health.

Because these disorders share overlapping symptoms—like fatigue, poor concentration, and restless nights—it can be difficult to know which one may be affecting you. Explanation

Sleep disorders can make it hard to get a good night's rest. Here are some common types:

Insomnia: This is when you have trouble falling asleep or staying asleep. It can make you feel very tired during the day.

Sleep Apnea: This is a condition where you stop breathing for short periods while you sleep. It can make you snore loudly and feel sleepy during the day.

Restless Legs Syndrome (RLS): This is when you have an uncomfortable feeling in your legs and an urge to move them, especially at night.

Narcolepsy: This disorder makes you feel very sleepy during the day and can cause sudden sleep attacks.
