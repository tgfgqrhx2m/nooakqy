2. Peptides for the win

5. Break a sweat

Everyone knows exercise is good for your health. Did you know it’s one of the strongest anti-aging warriors? Exercise can help you look and feel younger. It improves heart health and boosts circulation, which contributes toward hydrated, glowing skin.

Exercise for at least 3–4 times a week to keep your muscles fit and maintain joint flexibility. Regular sessions maintain a healthy weight and prevent the buildup of abdominal fat.

Regular aerobic exercises, like running and high-intensity interval training (HIIT), are anti-aging superstars. They elongate the telomeres, preventing cell death.
