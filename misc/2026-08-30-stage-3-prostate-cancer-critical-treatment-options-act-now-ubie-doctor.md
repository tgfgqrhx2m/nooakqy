What are the treatment options for stage 3 prostate cancer?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Stage 3 prostate cancer treatment typically includes three main options: surgery (radical prostatectomy), radiation therapy (external beam or brachytherapy), and hormone therapy (androgen deprivation therapy). These treatments are often combined for better outcomes. The best approach depends on tumor size, PSA levels, Gleason score, overall health, and personal preferences.
