About this lesson

This face-lift course teaches non-surgical lifting using Stylage XXL to address deep nasolabial folds, marionette lines, and mid-face descent.

Dr. Sopena's lateral lifting strategy provides structural support that reverses tissue dropping and restores youthful facial contours.

Watch real-time treatment demonstrating injection placement, immediate molding, and before-and-after comparisons from multiple viewing angles.
