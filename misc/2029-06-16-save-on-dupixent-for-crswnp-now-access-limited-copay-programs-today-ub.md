Explanation

Chronic Rhinosinusitis with Nasal Polyps (CRSwNP) affects thousands of people, causing persistent nasal congestion, loss of smell, facial pressure and recurrent infections. Dupixent® (dupilumab) has emerged as an effective biologic therapy to control symptoms and reduce polyp size. However, the cost of Dupixent for CRSwNP can be a barrier. This guide outlines practical steps, assistance programs and resources to help you manage out-of-pocket expenses without increasing stress.

Understanding the Cost of Dupixent for CRSwNP

List Price• Each two-mL syringe of Dupixent has a wholesale acquisition cost of approximately $3,100–$3,200.• Most patients require one injection every two weeks after an initial loading dose.

Insurance Coverage Variability• Private health plans, Medicare Part B or D and Medicaid may cover Dupixent differently.• Coverage depends on formulary status, step-therapy requirements and prior authorization rules.• Even with insurance, copays or coinsurance can range from $50 to over $1,000 per injection.

Factors Affecting Your Cost• Plan deductible and out-of-pocket maximum• Pharmacy benefit manager (PBM) protocols• In-network vs. out-of-network specialty pharmacies

Copay Assistance Options

1. Sanofi Patient Assistance Program (Sanofi MyWay®)

Sanofi, the manufacturer of Dupixent, offers the Sanofi MyWay® program to help eligible individuals reduce or eliminate copays.

Eligibility Criteria• U.S. resident with commercial insurance• Annual household income up to $100,000 (up to $150,000 for multi-person households)• Confirmation of insurance coverage and FDA-approved indication

Benefits• Copay assistance up to $10,000 per calendar year• Coordination with your specialty pharmacy for streamlined billing

How to Apply

Visit the Sanofi MyWay® website or call their dedicated helpline.

Submit proof of income, insurance card and physician prescription.

Receive an approval letter you share with your specialty pharmacy.

Patient Advocate Foundation• Case management and financial aid for copays and deductibles• Eligibility varies by program

NeedyMeds and RxAssist• Online directories of assistance programs by medication• Application guidance

Strategies to Minimize Out-of-Pocket Costs

Verify Formulary Status• Contact your insurer or PBM to confirm whether Dupixent is on the preferred drug list.• Ask if prior authorization or step therapy is required.

Explore Alternative Coverage• If uninsured or underinsured, consider state Medicaid programs or Affordable Care Act (ACA) marketplace plans.• Check if you qualify for Medicare Extra Help (Low-Income Subsidy) to reduce Part D costs.

Request a Financial Navigator• Many hospitals, clinics and nonprofit organizations employ financial navigators.• They can guide you through paperwork and identify additional aid.

Mail-Order vs. Retail Pharmacy• Mail-order specialty pharmacies may offer lower copays for 90-day supplies, if approved.• Compare costs between local specialty pharmacies and national mail-order options.

Plan for Deductible Seasons• If you meet your annual deductible early in the year, copays may drop afterward.• Strategize the timing of your first injection to maximize deductible benefits.

Tips for a Smooth Application Process

Gather Documentation Early: Proof of income, insurance info, prescription details and a recent medical record noting your CRSwNP diagnosis.

Work Closely with Your Healthcare Team: Physicians and nurses can submit prior authorizations and appeals letters supporting medical necessity.

Keep Detailed Records: Track submission dates, reference numbers and correspondence with foundations or insurers.

Follow Up Regularly: Check application status every 2–4 weeks to prevent delays.

Appeal Denials Quickly: If coverage is denied, request an internal appeal and appeal with your insurer's external review board if needed.
