We do not bill insurance companies for our services. We will provide a superbill for you so that you can present it to your insurance company. Insurance companies have been known to reimburse their clients for our services.

What are the benefits of IV mobile therapy in Denver?

There are several benefits, including faster recovery from your workouts, increased support for your immune system, a boost for your energy levels, increased hydration and detoxification. Mobile IV therapy is administered directly into your bloodstream, so it begins to work quickly without having to go through your digestive system. The added convenience of having your treatment in your own home, if you choose, makes mobile IV therapy an even better option than going to your doctor’s office.

Are there any side effects of IV therapy?

IV therapy is considered to be safe, but there are some risks associated with it. For example, you may experience inflammation, bruising or an infection at the injection site. Sometimes, people have an allergic reaction to this therapy, or they experience damage to their veins. If you have a medical condition, like heart failure or kidney disease, you may not be a candidate for IV therapy, so you must consult your physician first.
