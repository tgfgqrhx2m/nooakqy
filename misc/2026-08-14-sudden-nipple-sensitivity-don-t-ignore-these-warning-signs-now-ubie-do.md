Sudden nipple sensitivity is most commonly caused by hormonal changes, friction from clothing, early pregnancy, certain medications, or skin irritation such as eczema or contact dermatitis. In most cases, it is not serious and resolves on its own. However, persistent, worsening, or one-sided sensitivity — especially when paired with discharge, lumps, or skin changes — should be evaluated by a healthcare provider.

Explanation

Nipple sensitivity can appear suddenly and may feel like soreness, tingling, or heightened sensitivity to touch. Common causes include:

[Sensitivity Don Ignore These](https://regeneration-desk.hbsnzj.com/blog/7333779707.html)

Hormonal changes: Fluctuations due to menstruation, ovulation, or pregnancy often cause temporary sensitivity. Pregnancy: One of the earliest signs of pregnancy is nipple tenderness from increased blood flow and hormonal shifts. Friction or irritation: From tight clothing, exercise, or harsh soaps. Allergic reactions: To laundry detergents, lotions, or fabrics that come into contact with the skin. Medications: Certain medications, especially those that affect hormones, can trigger breast or nipple sensitivity. Infection or inflammation: Conditions like mastitis or eczema may cause tenderness, though these are often accompanied by other symptoms.

If nipple sensitivity is persistent, painful, or accompanied by discharge, skin changes, or a lump, you can check your symptoms using a free AI symptom checker to help understand what might be causing your discomfort before consulting a healthcare provider.
