# These Stylish Coats and Jackets Are All Less Than $300

Our product picks are editor-tested, expert-approved. We may earn a commission through links on our site. Why Trust Us?

There are so many options when it comes to outerwear that it's impossible not to find a jacket or coat that fits your fashion aesthetic without setting you back too much financially. We scoured some of our favorite online shops to hunt down a variety of styles all for less than $300. Need something to keep you dry in the rain? We got you. Something for the in-between weather of fall and spring? Still got you. Save a little money without sacrificing your style or comfort.

Ben Sherman Cord/Faux Suede Track Jacket

Get that retro vibe with a little twist. This jacket is straight from the runway at London Fashion Week and should go directly into your closet. The burgundy color fits well into any fall wardrobe collection.

VANS Drill Canvas Chore Coat

This offering from VANS has all the fun of the trendy camp shirt in jacket form. The chore coat is made from cotton canvas with corduroy trim. It's another one for any guy looking to achieve a retro look.

Howler Brothers Lightning Quilted Jacket

It may look thin, but this insulated quilted jacket will keep you warm and toasty well into the colder months. Part of the Howler capsule collection for Huckberry, these jackets are lightweight to make it easy to travel with, but durable to protect you from the elements.

Topman Classic Fit Peacoat

Every man should invest in a peacoat. It's an easy way to step up your winter look. This double-breasted coat from Topman delivers you the classic look at a totally reasonable price point.

Smartwool Anchor Line High Pile Fleece Lined Shirt Jacket

If you prefer your outerwear to look more like your favorite flannel shirt, look no further. It seems like every brand is hopping on the shirt jacket bandwagon and Smartwool has this merino blend option, ideal for camping or any other outdoor fun.

Bonobos Fleece Shirt Jacket

The fleece shirt jacket from Bonobos is essentially granting you permission to wear a blanket. That's kind of all you need to know.

Carhartt Men's Sandstone Active Jacket

Sometimes a heavy duty hoodie does the trick. The jacket's quilted lining takes it a step further for added warmth. The split kangaroo pocket in the front is also good for keeping your hands toasty and the internal zip pocket offers a little big of extra storage.

Eddie Bauer BC Uplift Jacket

When you're just looking for some light protection, try this one on for size. The bright color is also ideal for runners or bikers who need to be visible out on the road.

The North Face Venture 2 Jacket

Here's another option for anyone looking for something light and compact. It's windproof, waterproof, breathable—pretty much everything you've come to expect from a North Face jacket.

Taylor Stitch Long Haul Jacket

Embrace the seasonal shift that comes every April and September by busting out this washed chocolate herringbone. It's an item you can feel good about adding to your wardrobe, because it's also made from 100% BCI-certified cotton, meaning it was sourced from material grown under responsible agricultural practices.

Peregrine Waxed Bexley Jacket

Capture a vintage British military look from a brand that has been in the game for over two centuries. The Millerain cotton fabric is soft with a water-repellent finish and the top-notch quality becomes apparent the first time you slip it on.

Topo Designs Puffer Hoodie

Here's one for the eco-conscious shopper. This jacket will offer you protection from Mother Nature, while also making a difference for Mother Earth. The insulation is actually made from 100% recycled plastic bottles. With everything happening in the world today, this one should almost be a wardrobe requirement.

Uniqlo Reversible Parka

Not only is this a steal at the $50 price point, it's also two pieces in one. Whatever color you opt for on the outside, you could flip it and wear the jersey inner fabric instead. It's not easy to find a reversible item that looks as good both ways.

Mountain Hardwear Ghost Shadow Insulated Hoodie

This year should finally be the year you step into eco-friendly fashion. Nearly every component of this jacket is partially made all the way down to the zippers.

Scotch & Soda Lightweight Zip-Through Jacket

When that spring warmth starts kicking in, you should go lighter on the jacket and color. It's a basic style accentuated by the striped collar and drawstring hem.

Rye 51 Navy Faded Stripe Orville Bomber

There are countless variations on the classic bomber look, but this one will really help you elevate your wardrobe. Touching the distressed wool material made in Italy will make it worth every penny to drop a little more on this version.
