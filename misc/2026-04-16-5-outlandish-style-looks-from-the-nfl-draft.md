# 5 Outlandish Style Looks From the NFL Draft

By the time most professional athletes become household names, they have stylists to help dress them off the field.

But before they hit the big time, their style is less refined. Enter the 2016 NFL Draft, which was filled with some eye-popping ensembles. (We’re looking at you, Ezekiel Elliot.)

While these athletes’ outfits may not work for you on a daily basis, there are certain aspects of their looks that we love. Here’s how to steal some practical cues from their lavish looks.

Ezekiel Elliot (Drafted #4 to the Dallas Cowboys)

Elliot dominated the Draft red carpet by layering this blue and white suit over a cropped top.

Learn from the look: Skip the skimpy shirts, but keep the light suit. Use a contrast color, though—like a neutral button-front shirt that actually covers your belly button—to really make the white pop.

Darron Lee (#20, New York Jets)

There’s a lot happening here: a printed sport coat, an oversized lapel, and a bow tie. They’re all competing for the spotlight.

Learn from the look: Focus on one thing, like the print. Make that the main focus, and ease up on the other details.

Despite the busy ensemble, Lee totally nailed the fit. Notice how the top layer fits his natural shoulder, and there’s no puckering around his torso.

Kevin Dodd (#33, Tennessee Titans)

We’re digging the pattern on Dodd’s sport coat—but the thin lapels and red pocket square make it feel a little more black-tie affair than NFL Draft. Learn from the look: Take note of your events. For day-into-evening events, an understated, slim-fit suit is a better pick.

Vernon Hargreaves (#11, Tampa Bay Buccaneers)

Hargreaves rocked this standout suit on Draft night. He nailed the perfect pant length, and we’re digging the cognac shoe contrast.

But a bracelet, watch, and necklace are overkill.

Learn from the look: For a dressier occasion, sport a statement watch and ditch the other accessories.

Jalen Ramsey (#5, Jacksonville Jaguars)

From bedazzled midsoles to floral prints, we loved looking at Ramsey’s footwear choices. However, his collection was all over the map during Draft week.

Learn from the look: There’s a time and a place for statement shoes. For Ramsey, it’s the NFL Draft. For you, it’s a summer wedding.
