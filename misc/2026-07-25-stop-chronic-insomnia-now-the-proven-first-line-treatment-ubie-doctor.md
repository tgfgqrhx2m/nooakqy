What is the first-line treatment for chronic insomnia?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Chronic insomnia is best treated with Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia (CBT-I), which is the recommended first-line treatment by leading sleep medicine guidelines. CBT-I is preferred over sleep medications because it addresses the root causes of insomnia, produces lasting, long-term results, and avoids the side effects and dependency risks associated with sleeping pills.

Key benefits of CBT-I include:

Long-lasting improvements in sleep quality

No medication side effects

Explanation

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia, or CBT-I, is considered the best first treatment for chronic insomnia. This is because it helps people change their sleep habits and thoughts about sleep. Unlike medications, CBT-I teaches skills that can help improve sleep over the long term.

Why CBT-I?

Effective: Research shows that CBT-I works well for many people with chronic insomnia.

Long-lasting: The skills learned in CBT-I can help improve sleep even after the therapy ends.

No side effects: Unlike some medicines, CBT-I does not have side effects.
