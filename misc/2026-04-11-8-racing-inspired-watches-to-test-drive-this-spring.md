There’s movement, as in the mechanism that powers a watch’s timekeeping ability—lots of options there. And then there’s movement, as in speedy, racing-inspired style, which only a bold face and a striped-fabric band can give a watch. Check out the coolest watch trend for the man that has a need for speed.

Tommy Hilfiger Men's Navy Silicone Strap 46mm Watch

For under $100, you can sport the racing-inspired watch trend with Tommy Hilfiger’s silicone strap stunner. In the brand’s signature colors, this handsome watch is great for showing some patriotic flair on July 4th.

CitIzen Eco-Drive PRT Watch

Perfect to sport daily with your wardrobe of neutrals, this racing-watch features a cool blue-tone stainless steel watch case and striped NATO strap for a look that’s both sleek and sporty. The Eco-Drive technology allows this watch to be powered by light, which means you actually save not having to spend on replacement batteries year-after-year.

Timex + Todd Snyder Black Maritime Sport Watch

Just one of the many great offerings from the ongoing collaboration between Timex and Todd Snyder, the Maritime Sport watch has a 41-millimeter case and double-layer nylon strap with a cool Indigo Night-Light Feature.
