Internal Medicine

Mood affective disorders are mental health conditions that cause significant disturbances in a person's emotional state, leading to prolonged periods of extreme sadness, elevated mood, or rapid shifts between the two. Common types include major depressive disorder, bipolar disorder, and cyclothymia. These conditions can impact daily functioning, relationships, sleep, and overall quality of life.

If you're noticing persistent changes in your mood, energy, or motivation, it's worth understanding what may be behind them. Symptoms of mood affective disorders often overlap with other medical and mental health conditions, which makes self-diagnosis unreliable and delays effective treatment.
