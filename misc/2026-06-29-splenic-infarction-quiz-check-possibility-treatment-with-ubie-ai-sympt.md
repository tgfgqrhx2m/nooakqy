What is Splenic Infarction?

Splenic infarction is a condition where blood flow to the spleen is reduced or blocked, typically by a clot, causing tissue death due to oxygen shortage.

Typical Symptoms of Splenic Infarction

Yellowish eyes or skin

Stomach pain that worsens when the stomach is pressed

Pain in the upper left abdomen

Weakness in your arms or legs

Fever

Feels a hard lump in upper right abdomen

Stomach or abdominal pain

Chest tightness

Diagnostic Questions for Splenic Infarction

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Have you noticed any yellowing of your eyes or skin?

Does your stomach pain get worse when you press on it?

Are you experiencing pain in the upper left abdomen?

Do you have weakness in your arms or legs?

Do you have a fever?

Treatment of Splenic Infarction

Initial management includes hydration, painkillers, and monitoring. In many cases, symptoms resolve in 7-14 days. Splenectomy may be done for persistent symptoms or complications.
