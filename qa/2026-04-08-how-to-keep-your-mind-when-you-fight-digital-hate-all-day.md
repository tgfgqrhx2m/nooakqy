# How to Keep Your Mind When You Fight Digital Hate All Day

ONLINE OUTRAGE CULTURE isn’t slowing down, so neither is Imran Ahmed, 45, CEO of the Center for Countering Digital Hate, which exposes hate-mongers and pressures platforms to reform. Among its projects: uncovering the Disinformation Dozen—the 12 people behind 65 percent of online vaccine misinformation. In aiming to counter hate, Ahmed gets a lot of it. Here’s how he manages:

5:00 a.m.

[Your Mind When You](https://synaptide-lab.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/4432764588.html)

Triage

Since we also operate in the UK and Europe, I’ve got to be 100 percent when I wake up—there are always messages needing attention first thing. I identify items that need action and address urgent messages. After that, it’s coffee, showering, dressing, and saying good morning to my wife.

5:45 a.m.

Decisions, Decisions

Everything for me is oriented around being able to make great decisions and communicate them effectively. I like to take on decisions first thing, without bringing the baggage of a day into them, since decision fatigue accrues throughout the day.

7:00 a.m.

[How to](https://mindpeptide.bbsxyxy.com/blog/1246378836.html)

Skip Scrolling

I seek information that helps me make decisions, and consume very little from the digital flow. Consuming from algorithmically generated timelines on social media that are designed to keep us hooked is like drinking from a stream at the back of your house. Sure, the water looks nice, but you don’t know who’s put what in it. It can make you really sick.

11:00 a.m.

[Mind When You](https://glp-insights.xianfengchem.com/blog/9246221700.html)

Work Out

[When You](https://brainmessenger.xianfengchem.com/)

I carve out 30 minutes a day for exercise. Sometimes it’s resistance bands and bodyweight exercises between meetings.

2:00 p.m.

[How to](https://aegispeptide.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/1515208806.html)

[Hate All Day](https://satietybrief.china-akan.com/blog/9271186899.html)

Respond, Don’t React

When you do something impactful, you have to expect a negative consequence alongside your positive one. There will be people who want to cheer everything you do and others who want to end your life. You’ve got to not let it affect you. I try not to care when someone attacks me. I just care about whether I’m using both my hands to push the arc of history toward justice.

3:00 p.m.

Take a Break

Midafternoon energy became a challenge when I turned 40. So I take mini breaks. If it’s been a really brutal day, I might watch a few minutes of something on Netflix. I love dumb movies where the good guy wins (Marvel, Michael Bay explode-a-thons). I think that’s because I love the idea of good defeating evil and light illuminating darkness.

6:00 to 8:00 p.m.

[How to Keep Your](https://beautybiometrics.hbsnzj.com/blog/2503818814.html)

Leave Work

I switch off to spend time with my wife. I’m lucky that my work doesn’t linger in my mind in my spare time.

[Your Mind](https://musclemend.sunsimiaosd.com/)

[Keep Your](https://brainmessenger.xianfengchem.com/blog/0647705722.html)

10:00 p.m.

Get Sleep

I am deeply anxious about what happens to the world, to our democracy, and to our kids if we fail as an organization, but I know I do everything I can each day to counter the things I fear, so I sleep peacefully.
