Narcolepsy is a type of sleep disorder. The most common symptom is excessive daytime sleepiness, where you feel very tired during the day even after a good night's sleep. Excessive daytime sleepiness can also cause you to fall asleep in the middle of eating, working, or talking with someone.

[Narcolepsy Warning Signs](https://immunopeptide.xianfengchem.com/)

[Now Ubie Doctor's Note](https://brainmessenger.xianfengchem.com/blog/0085741544.html)

Other symptoms can affect people with narcolepsy, but not everyone with narcolepsy will experience the following symptoms: cataplexy (which is a sudden loss of muscle control, often triggered by strong emotions like laughter, surprise, or anger); sleep paralysis (which is when a person briefly can't move or speak when falling asleep or waking up); and hallucinations (which are vivid dream-like experiences -- for people with narcolepsy, hallucinations tend to happen as people fall asleep or wake up).

To be certain about a diagnosis of narcolepsy, you will need to consult a healthcare provider. This healthcare provider will provide a personalized assessment of your symptoms. Also, the healthcare provider may recommend sleep-related testing that would confirm a diagnosis of narcolepsy.
