What might cause my urine stream to lose strength?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

A weak urine stream happens when urine flows more slowly or with less force than normal. Common causes include stress, side effects from certain medications, and underlying health conditions such as an enlarged prostate, urinary tract infections, bladder issues, or nerve problems. Identifying the cause is key to finding the right treatment and preventing complications.

Because a weak urine stream can point to many possible conditions—some minor and some more serious—it's important not to guess. Another reason could be medications. Some drugs may cause bladder issues, affecting how you pee (Reference 2). If you started a new medication and noticed changes, it might be worth discussing with your doctor.

Lastly, certain health conditions can impact urine flow. For example, some treatments like tadalafil have been studied for their effects on urine flow (Reference 3).
