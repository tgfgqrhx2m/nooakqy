What could a slow urine flow mean?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Slow urine flow, also called urinary hesitancy, is when the stream of urine is weaker or slower than normal. It often signals a problem with the bladder, urethra, prostate, or the nerves that control urination. Common causes include an enlarged prostate, urinary tract infections, bladder muscle weakness, nerve damage, or blockages in the urinary tract.

Because slow urine flow can point to several different conditions, identifying the underlying cause is key to getting the right treatment.
