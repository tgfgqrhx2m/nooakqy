When you experience tingling throughout your whole body for a long time, it’s a sign that it is important to get a checkup. This tingling may be linked to problems with your nerves or other health conditions that could affect your quality of life. A doctor can ask you questions and examine you to see if there is an underlying cause such as hyperesthesia or other nerve issues. They might run tests, like blood work or nerve studies, to help them understand what is happening. In some cases, changes in lifestyle, physical therapy, or medications can help reduce your symptoms. It is always a good idea to keep track of when the tingling happens, how long it lasts, and if anything makes it better or worse. This information can help the doctor decide what to do next. Remember, while the feeling may not be dangerous, only a health professional can say for sure and help guide you to feel better.
