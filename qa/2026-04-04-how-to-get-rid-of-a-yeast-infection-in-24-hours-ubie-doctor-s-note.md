Rapid relief from a vaginal yeast infection is possible within 24 hours. The most effective options are a single 150 mg oral fluconazole tablet or an OTC intravaginal azole such as tioconazole or miconazole. Complete the full recommended course even if symptoms improve quickly.

Before treating, consider key factors: pregnancy status, potential medication interactions, correct application technique, and supportive care like wearing breathable cotton underwear and avoiding douching. If symptoms persist beyond 48–72 hours, follow up with a healthcare provider.

How to Get Rid of a Yeast Infection in 24 Hours

Yeast infections (vulvovaginal candidiasis) affect up to 75% of people with vaginas at least once in their lifetime. While complete eradication of a yeast infection may take several days, you can significantly reduce itching, burning, and discharge within 24 hours by following evidence-based treatments and supportive measures.

1. Understand Your Yeast Infection

Common symptoms:

Itching and irritation around the vulva

Thick, white "cottage cheese" discharge

Redness and swelling of vulvar tissues

Burning during urination or intercourse

When to seek immediate care:

Fever, chills, or lower abdominal pain

Unusual or foul-smelling discharge

First episode over age 40 or after menopause

Diabetes, pregnancy, or a weakened immune system

2. First-Line Treatments for Rapid Relief

A. Single-Dose Oral Fluconazole

(Clinical practice guideline: Pappas et al., Clin Infect Dis 2016; Sobel et al., Obstet Gynecol 1999)

Dose: 150 mg taken once by mouth

Onset of action: Many people report reduced itching and burning within 24 hours

One-time dose

No intravaginal application needed

Do not take if nursing an infant under 2 months

May interact with other medications (e.g., warfarin, certain antiepileptics)

Consult your doctor if you have liver disease

B. Topical Azole Antifungals

(2016 IDSA guideline; Workowski & Bolan, MMWR 2015)

Clotrimazole 1% cream (5- to 7-day course)

Miconazole 2% cream (3- to 7-day course)

Tioconazole 6.5% ointment (single-dose intravaginal)

Apply at bedtime for best distribution

Follow package instructions precisely

Continue therapy for the full recommended duration, even if symptoms improve

Many users feel symptom relief within 24 hours of the first application

Tip: Avoid combining different topical treatments without medical advice

3. Supportive Measures to Speed Recovery

Wear breathable, loose-fitting cotton underwear

Avoid tight jeans or synthetic fabrics

Skip douching, scented soaps, bath oils, and sprays

Change out of wet swimsuits or workout clothes promptly

Use a clean, soft towel to pat (not rub) the area dry

Consider a sitz bath in warm (not hot) water for 10–15 minutes
