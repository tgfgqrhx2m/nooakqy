Sore breasts are one of the earliest and most common signs of pregnancy. Rising levels of estrogen, progesterone, and hCG shortly after conception increase blood flow and fluid retention in breast tissue, often causing tenderness, swelling, tingling, or heightened sensitivity as early as one to two weeks after conception.

Explanation

During early pregnancy, many women experience sore breasts. This is a common symptom that happens because the body is preparing for breastfeeding.

[This Doctor](https://pigmentlab.jcesqc8.com/blog/3456482787.html)

Why Do Breasts Get Sore?

Hormonal Changes: Hormones like estrogen and progesterone increase, causing changes in the breast tissue.

Breast Growth: The breasts may grow larger and feel tender as they get ready to produce milk.

Increased Blood Flow: More blood flows to the breasts, which can also cause soreness.

These changes are a normal part of pregnancy as the body adjusts to support a growing baby.
