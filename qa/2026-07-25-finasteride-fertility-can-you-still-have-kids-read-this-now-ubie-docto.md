Can I still have kids if I’ve been on finasteride for years?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Long-term finasteride use may impact male fertility, though effects vary from person to person. Finasteride, commonly prescribed for hair loss and enlarged prostate, can lower sperm count and reduce semen quality in some men. The good news: these effects are often reversible after stopping the medication. If you're planning to conceive or have concerns about fertility while taking finasteride, consult your doctor to discuss alternatives or temporary discontinuation.
