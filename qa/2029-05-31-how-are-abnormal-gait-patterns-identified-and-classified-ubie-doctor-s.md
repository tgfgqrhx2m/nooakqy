Explanation

[Abnormal Gait Patterns](https://yldzdh.com/fdbi4rnu/)

Medical professionals begin by observing a person's walking pattern, studying their steps and body movements. They may supplement these observations with specialized equipment—cameras, sensors, and computer software—to measure specific aspects like step length, speed, and joint angles. They document characteristics including uneven weight distribution, reduced step length, and irregular knee and hip movements. By comparing these features to established patterns, clinicians can identify specific gait types, including "shuffling," "Parkinsonian," and "ataxic," among others. Based on this classification, doctors can identify underlying causes and create targeted treatment and therapy plans.
