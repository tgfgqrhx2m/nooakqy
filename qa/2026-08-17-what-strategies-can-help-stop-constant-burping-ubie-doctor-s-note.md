Constant burping can be bothersome, but there are ways to manage it. One effective strategy is diaphragmatic breathing, which involves deep breathing using the diaphragm. This technique has been shown to reduce belching by helping control the air swallowed.

Additionally, behavioral changes can play a crucial role. These might include modifying eating habits, such as eating slowly and avoiding talking while eating, to reduce the amount of air swallowed.
