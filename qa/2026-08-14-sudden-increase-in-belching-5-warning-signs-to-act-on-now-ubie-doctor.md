Sudden, excessive belching is most often caused by aerophagia (swallowing too much air) or an underlying digestive issue such as acid reflux (GERD), gastritis, indigestion (dyspepsia), or an H. pylori infection.

Common causes of a sudden increase in belching include:

Eating or drinking too quickly

Carbonated beverages, chewing gum, or smoking

Anxiety or stress-induced air swallowing

Acid reflux or hiatal hernia

Gastritis or peptic ulcers

Food intolerances (lactose, gluten, fructose)

While occasional belching is normal, a sudden change in frequency can signal something worth investigating—especially if paired with heartburn, bloating, nausea, or abdominal pain. Because the causes range from simple habits to conditions that benefit from treatment, identifying the likely trigger early helps you take the right next step.
