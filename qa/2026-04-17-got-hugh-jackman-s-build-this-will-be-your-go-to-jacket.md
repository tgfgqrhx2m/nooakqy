We’re calling it: Wolverine is a style icon. OK, maybe not Wolverine, but Hugh Jackman certainly is. The 48-year-old actor turned up in style to a New York Jets game earlier this week, wearing a henley, shearling jacket, and aviators.

Jackman's outfit here is practical, stylish, and perfect for fall and winter. It's also ideal for game day, when the weather might start mild, but gets cooler as the sun goes down. Want to replicate the look? Retire that oversized sweatshirt or jersey, and embrace Jackman's polished and—most importantly—well-fitting look.

The Australian actor clearly knows how to dress his muscular build. He chose a black leather jacket with shearling details as the centerpiece of the look, which in addition to looking cool as hell, will also keep you warm. The pebbled leather detailing on the jacket gives it a more laid-back feel, fit for the weekend or even over your suit. His just-the-right-size coat helps emphasize his broad shoulders, rather than hide them under an oversized parka, and the shearling collar balances out the jacket.

Underneath, Jackman wore an understated, neutral henley,which is also always a good choice because it pairs nicely with just about anything. Comfortable and easy to dress up or down, it’s a closet staple that every man needs. Like the perfect t-shirt, a henley flatters your torso and looks great on its own, or layered under a jacket or sweater.

The final look is polished, but still appropriate for a casual evening spent cheering on your team (from the field, by the look of Jackman’s Instagram post).
