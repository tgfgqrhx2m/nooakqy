Explanation

Nipples can vary in size, shape, and firmness from one side to the other. Factors influencing this include:

Natural asymmetry: It's normal for one nipple to be slightly different in firmness or size compared to the other. Temperature changes: Cold or stimulation (like touch or sexual arousal) can cause nipples to become erect or firmer. Hormonal influences: Hormone fluctuations during menstrual cycles, pregnancy, or breastfeeding can affect nipple sensitivity and firmness. Physical stimulation: Rubbing against clothing or other surfaces can temporarily make one nipple feel harder.

If one nipple feels persistently hard, painful, or if there are changes in color, size, discharge, or lumps, you can use our free symptom checker to help assess your symptoms and determine whether you should see a healthcare provider for evaluation.
