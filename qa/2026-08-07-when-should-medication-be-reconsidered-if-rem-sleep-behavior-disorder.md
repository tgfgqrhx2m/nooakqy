When should medication be reconsidered if REM Sleep Behavior Disorder (RBD) leads to physical injury?

Internal Medicine

Medication for REM Sleep Behavior Disorder (RBD) should be reconsidered when it fails to prevent physical injuries during sleep. Because RBD causes people to physically act out vivid dreams, effective treatment is essential to protect both the patient and their bed partner from harm. If current medications aren't working, a doctor may adjust the dose, switch prescriptions (such as between clonazepam and melatonin), or recommend safety modifications to the sleep environment.

If you're experiencing injuries, unusual sleep behaviors, or suspect RBD may be affecting you or a loved one, don't wait to seek clarity. Untreated RBD can worsen over time and has been linked to neurological conditions, making early evaluation critical.
