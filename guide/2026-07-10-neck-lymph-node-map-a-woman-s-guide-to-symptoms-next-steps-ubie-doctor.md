What Are Lymph Nodes and Why Do They Matter?

Lymph nodes are small, bean-shaped glands that are part of your immune system. They filter lymph fluid and help your body fight infections, viruses, and even abnormal cells.

When your body detects an infection or inflammation, lymph nodes can:

Swell

Feel tender

Become more noticeable to the touch

Most of the time, this swelling is temporary and harmless. But in some cases, persistent or unusual changes need medical evaluation.

Where Are Lymph Nodes Located in the Neck?

If you're wondering where are lymph nodes located in neck, they are found throughout several key areas. You can't normally see or feel healthy lymph nodes, but when they enlarge, they may become noticeable.

Why Do Neck Lymph Nodes Swell?

The most common causes include:

✅ Infections (Most Common)

Common cold

COVID-19

Dental abscess

Skin infections

Mononucleosis

✅ Inflammatory Conditions

Autoimmune disorders (such as lupus or rheumatoid arthritis)

✅ Less Common but Serious Causes

Lymphoma

Leukemia

Metastatic cancer (spread from another area)

Tuberculosis

While cancer is possible, it is far less common than infection—especially if the lymph node is painful and appeared during an illness.

Special Considerations for Women

Women may notice neck lymph node changes related to:

Hormonal shifts

Autoimmune conditions (more common in women)

Thyroid disorders

Breast cancer (rarely spreads to supraclavicular nodes)

If you have a history of cancer, any new or persistent lymph node swelling should be evaluated promptly.
