What to Consider

Movement

The movement is the engine that powers the watch and determines how it keeps time. Under $1,000 you’ll typically find watch brands use automatic, manual and quartz options:

Automatic

Winds itself using your wrist’s motion; runs without a battery as long as you wear it.

Manual

Needs to be wound by hand; offers a classic, traditional feel.

Quartz

Battery-powered and highly accurate, with minimal maintenance.

Case Diameter

Case diameter determines how large the watch appears on your wrist and plays a major role in how comfortable it is and how stylish it looks. Most versatile everyday watches fall between 38mm and 41mm, which tends to look balanced on most guys’ wrists.

Water Resistance

Water resistance determines how well the watch can handle moisture and everyday exposure. A rating of at least 100 is ideal for everyday wear, whisk dive watches often reach 200 meters or more for serious aquatic use.
